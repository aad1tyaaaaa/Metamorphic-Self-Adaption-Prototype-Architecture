What this page does and does not do. The Task Analyzer heuristics, the f_theta forward pass (exported weights, 8→32→16→3), the confidence policy and the stability monitor are reimplemented here and genuinely execute on your input. Every loss, perplexity, latency and accuracy figure is a recorded measurement replayed from results/. This page does not run GPT-2.

Measured limitations.

  • Full MSA is the worst-quality variant tested; attention and FFN slicing are applied to pretrained weights with no retraining.
  • Calibration targets come from prompt loss, which is not downstream task quality.
  • Latency was measured on CPU; the routing baseline uses less arithmetic yet runs slower.
  • Evaluation sets are small (60 short-QA, 40 GSM8K); small differences are not meaningful.
